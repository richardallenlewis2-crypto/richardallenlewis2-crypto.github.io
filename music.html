ABOUT THE MUSIC

The music is made to lower the noise. Slow, steady songs, written to take the edge off a hard day — calm by design, not by accident.

Every song starts as words on paper. The writing comes first — lyrics written by a songwriter with decades of practice behind him. Some recordings are produced with Suno as a studio tool. The songs themselves are written by a person.
