ABOUT

I'm an artist and designer. My core work is drawn by hand — pencil studies, blackwork, and dark fantasy art from my own world, The Undersung. I also design posters, print and workbook layouts, and clean one-page websites, hand-coded in HTML and CSS.

Everything below is my own work: the drawings are hand-drawn and scanned from paper, the posters and print pieces are designed by me, and every website is hand-coded — no page builders, no templates.
